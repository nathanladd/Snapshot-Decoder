Battery Voltage

PIDs Used in This Chart

PID Name Description Units
P_L_Battery_raw Battery voltage Volts
IN_Engine_cycle_speed Engine speed RPM

Background

For a 12V system, battery voltage is generally maintained between 12 – 15V while the engine is running. The ECU can operate normally above 6V. If battery voltage drops too low — as can happen during a cold start — power-related faults may occur.

Key thresholds
  • Normal running  12 – 15V
  • ECU minimum  Above 6V
  • Problem range  Below 9V during cranking — battery needs testing

How to Use This Quick Chart

This chart is most useful for diagnosing an engine that will not start. The ECU requires a minimum cranking speed before it will begin to deliver fuel — and that cranking speed depends heavily on battery voltage.

Step 1 — Load your snapshot and open the Battery Voltage Quick Chart.
Step 2 — Enable the Time Slider.

Use the Time Slider to scrub through the snapshot and monitor the engine state in real time.

Step 3 — Observe engine speed while the engine state is Cranking.

Watch both battery voltage and engine RPM together. A healthy crank will reach sufficient speed before the ECU enables fuel delivery.

What to Look For

Typical Start Sequence Normal

Battery voltage drops to around 10V while the engine cranks at approximately 369 RPM. After the engine fires, voltage climbs back to a healthy charging voltage around 14.11V.

This is the expected pattern — a brief voltage sag during cranking followed by a recovery once the alternator takes over.

Weak Battery — Cranking Failure Problem

The ECU can operate above 6V, but at only 8.5V this engine could not achieve the minimum cranking speed needed for the ECU to deliver fuel. The engine will not start.

Action required: This battery needs load testing. A battery that cannot sustain adequate voltage under the cranking load must be replaced before further diagnosis.
Note: Battery voltage problems can generate multiple fault codes — especially power-supply or sensor-reference faults. Always confirm battery health before chasing fault codes on an engine that would not start.
