PID Details

P_L_Egr_feedback_pos_cnts — EGR Valve Position

The operating range of the EGR valve is 6mm, with a sensor resolution of 0.5V/mm or 102 counts/mm.

P_L_Egr_close_pos_mean_nvv — EGR Closed Position Learning Value

This PID represents the ECU's learned closed position for the EGR valve. It drifts over time due to carbon buildup or contamination.

ConditionCountsVoltage

P0C18 — EGR Close Position Learning Range Fault
Set when the learning value drops below 690 counts (3.37V) — typically caused by carbon buildup or foreign matter preventing full closure.
P0C17 — EGR Close Position Learning Drift Fault
Set when the learning value drifts more than 100 counts (0.49V / 0.98mm) from its baseline — also caused by carbon or foreign matter.
IN_Egr_position — EGR Position Percent

Reports EGR valve position as 0 – 100%. The value itself is less important than whether feedback follows demand.

Compare IN_Egr_position (feedback) against ACM_Egr_position_dmnd (demand). If the position error exceeds 5% for more than 20 seconds, fault P0404 (EGR Position Control Fault) will set.

P0404 is grouped with many other EGR position-related faults in MyMachine.

How to Use This Quick Chart

This chart is used to evaluate EGR valve calibration health — specifically whether the valve's actual closed position still matches its learned closed position.

Step 1 — Load your snapshot and open the EGR Calibration Quick Chart.

The chart plots P_L_Egr_feedback_pos_cnts and P_L_Egr_close_pos_mean_nvv on the same axis.

Step 2 — Find a period when the EGR valve is closed.

During closed periods, the two lines should match closely. The actual position (feedback) and the learned closed position should overlap.

Step 3 — Watch the gap as the valve opens.

As the EGR valve opens, the actual position count decreases while the learned closed position stays fixed. The distance between the two lines reflects how far the valve has opened.

What to Look For
  • Lines that match at closed — healthy calibration
  • Lines that diverge at closed — carbon buildup is preventing full closure, learning value has drifted
  • Learning value trending below 690 cnts — approaching or past fault threshold
Diagnostic tip: The EGR valve circuit can be diagnosed from the ECU connector, which is more accessible than the valve itself. An H-Bridge circuit drives the EGR motor — faults in that circuit produce EGR position or motor-related codes.
